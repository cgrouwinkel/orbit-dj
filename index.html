HOW TO PLAYNotes fall down four lanes. They are the song’s real kick, snare, hat and FX hits, so tap the lane or its pad as each note crosses the line. Keys: F J K L, Space to start/pause, 1–8 for beat pads.
HOW YOU’RE GRADEDRhythm accuracy gives up to 700 points and equipment tasks up to 300. Grades: S 900+, A 750+, B 550+, C 350+.
WHAT’S REALTiming is judged on the Web Audio clock. The backing tracks are original synthesized layers. The crossfader blends layers of one track; true two-deck mixing is on the roadmap.
