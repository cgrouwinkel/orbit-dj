# Rigs research (2026-11-20)

What the owner asked for: the controllers and devices used by Fred again.., Skepta and artists near them in sound (UK garage, grime, house, rap production), built into ORBIT. This file records what was found, how confident it is, and how each finding maps to the app. Artists change gear often; treat every line as "reported at the time of the source".

## Limits

- Equipboard is community-built. It was used only where a video, photo or quote was cited.
- The in-app text names products as facts about real gear. ORBIT uses no logos, artwork, samples or presets from any of them.
- The DJ moves (reload, echo out, bass kill) are described from general knowledge of how grime, garage and rap sets are played, not from a cited source.
